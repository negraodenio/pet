💡 1. The Core Market Thesis
Why Compawion wins against legacy pet cameras and uncomfortable wearables
❌ The Wearable Failure
Smart collars (Fi, Whistle) irritate pets, require constant battery charging, and cause friction with elderly or anxious pets who reject collars indoors.

Compawion Solution: 100% Invisible Bed Sensors (Zero Wearables).

❌ The Camera Fatigue
Standard cameras (Furbo, Ring) blast owners with 50+ useless push notifications daily for wind, shadows, and robot vacuums. Pet parents eventually turn them off.

Compawion Solution: Edge AI alerts only for real medical signs (coughing, retching, limping).

❌ Late Diagnosis Tragedy
Dogs and cats biologically hide pain. By the time a senior pet visibly limps or vomits, congestive heart failure or renal failure has often progressed too far.

Compawion Solution: Continuous night respiration rate (SRR) alerts vets weeks ahead.
