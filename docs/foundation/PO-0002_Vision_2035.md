# 🌍 PO-0002 — VISION 2035

> **Document ID**: PO-0002  
> **Category**: Foundation  
> **Status**: Approved Strategy  

---

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

By 2035, PROJECT ONE will be the global digital twin platform for 500M+ companion animals worldwide. Every pet will have a continuous, real-time AI guardian monitoring health, behavior, nutrition, and longevity.

---

## 2. THE DECADE HORIZON

### Phase 1: Sensor & Vision Layer (2026 – 2027)
- Launch Vision Device & AI Station (Tablet).
- Establish **PDP 1.0** (Pet Device Protocol) & **PDL** (Pet Description Language).
- Achieve 100k active monitored pets.

### Phase 2: Biological & Environmental Ecosystem (2028 – 2030)
- Launch Smart Bed (Heart rate, respiration, pressure mapping, weight).
- Expand PDP to 3rd-party hardware manufacturers via open SDK.
- Deploy Autonomous Behavioral Intervention (Acoustic & voice calming algorithms).

### Phase 3: Predictive Clinical Twin & Global Network (2031 – 2035)
- Clinical-grade predictive veterinary AI: Detect early renal disease, osteoarthritis, and cardiac anomalies 6 months before symptoms appear.
- Universal Pet Passport & Vet Marketplace integration.

---

## 3. CORE METRIC
- **Primary Metric**: *Peace of Mind Score (POMS)* & *Pet Life Expectancy Extension*.
