# 📜 PO-0001 — PROJECT ONE MANIFESTO

> **Codename**: PROJECT ONE  
> **Core Principle**: *"We are not building a product. We are building peace of mind for millions of pet owners."*  
> **Strategic Insight**: *"We are not building hardware. We are building a universal language for intelligent pet devices."*

---

## 1. WHO WE ARE

PROJECT ONE is not a pet camera company.  
PROJECT ONE is not an IoT hardware company.  
PROJECT ONE is an **AI Intelligence & Universal Protocol Company for Companion Animals**.

Hardware is merely a sensor.  
The camera is one sensor. The Smart Bed is one sensor. The Smart Collar is one sensor.  
Everything feeds **One AI Brain**.

---

## 2. THE THREE PILLARS

1. **Peace of Mind First**: Every UX, AI, and hardware decision must answer: *"Does this increase the owner's peace of mind?"* If the answer is no, it does not belong in the product.
2. **Invisible Technology**: Technology disappears. AI operates autonomously in the background, calming pets and understanding behavior before disturbing human owners.
3. **Universal Pet Ecosystem (PDP & PDL)**: We create the global standard protocol (**Pet Device Protocol - PDP 1.0**) and biological data schema (**Pet Description Language - PDL**) connecting all pet hardware into a unified digital twin.

---

## 3. THE LONG-TERM MOAT

Every new device improves the AI.  
Every AI improvement benefits every customer.  
We are building the world's largest behavioral & physiological dataset for companion animals.
