# 📜 MANIFESTO.md — The Project One Manifesto

> **Motto**: *"We are not building a product. We are building peace of mind for millions of pet owners."*

---

## 1. OUR BELIEF

Companion animals are not pets. They are family.  
They share our homes, our routines, our joy, and our grief. Yet, when we step outside our front doors, they enter a silent world where their distress, pain, and needs are unobserved and unheard.

Traditional technology has failed companion animals.  
Consumer electronics companies have built cheap surveillance cameras that spam human owners with meaningless motion notifications. They treat pets like security risks in a parking lot.

We refuse to build surveillance cameras. We refuse to build noisy gadgets.  
We believe companion animals deserve **intelligence, dignity, and active care**.

---

## 2. THE ETHICAL IMPERATIVES

### 1. Technology Must Disappear
Technology should never demand attention. True intelligence is quiet, warm, and invisible. The guardian should never think "I have cameras installed." They should feel "My pet is safe."

### 2. Autonomous Intervention Over Human Alarmism
A pet experiencing separation anxiety or barking at a door does not need an alert sent to a busy human at work. They need immediate comfort. Our AI must attempt gentle, non-invasive intervention—playing soothing acoustic frequencies or recorded owner voices—before disturbing human guardians.

### 3. Absolute Privacy & Trust
Our homes are sacred. raw video and sensitive family data must be guarded with enterprise-grade encryption and PostgreSQL Row Level Security. Data is used exclusively to protect the animal, never for invasive advertising.

### 4. Explainable AI (XAI)
No black box algorithms. Every alert, intervention, or health score metric generated by the AI Brain must provide human-readable causal reasoning. Guardians must understand *why* the AI acted.

### 5. Multi-Decade Commitment
Short-term thinking produces disposable hardware. We build Project One to endure for decades. The digital memories, behavioral profiles, and health records created today will protect companion animals for their entire lives.

---

## 3. OUR PROMISE TO GUARDIANS AND ANIMALS

We promise to honor the bond between humans and companion animals.  
We promise to design with love, engineering rigor, and quiet elegance.  
We promise to measure our success solely by the peace of mind we create.
