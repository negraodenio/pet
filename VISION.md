## 1. PURPOSE & NORTH STAR

### The Purpose
Project One exists to improve and protect the lives of companion animals through trustworthy artificial intelligence.

### The North Star
By 2035, Project One will provide a continuous, non-invasive digital twin and cognitive AI guardian for 500M+ companion animals globally.

We define success not by hardware units sold, but by **Peace of Mind Delivered (POMD)** and **Companion Life Expectancy Extension (CLEE)**.

## 3. TECHNOLOGY VISION

### 3.1 Neural Micro-Cortices at the Edge
By 2030, vision and acoustic feature extraction will execute locally on sub-watt edge neural processors (TPUs) embedded directly into Vision Devices and Smart Beds, rendering local privacy absolute while streaming zero raw video to the cloud.

### 3.2 Predictive Veterinary Intelligence
By fusing non-invasive Ballistocardiography (BCG) heart rate variability from Smart Beds, posture kinematics from Vision Devices, and activity scores from Smart Collars, Project One AI will predict feline renal failure, canine osteoarthritis, and cardiac valve degeneration 6 to 12 months before clinical symptoms manifest.

---

## 4. ECOSYSTEM STRATEGY

### 4.1 Hardware Ecosystem
An expansive matrix of non-invasive sensors:
- **Vision Device**: 4K optical & infrared thermal sensing.
- **Smart Bed**: BCG vitals, respiration, weight, piezoresistive pressure mapping.
- **AI Station Tablet**: Always-on ambient home presence.
- **Smart Collar**: Micro-kinematics & GPS.
- **Smart Feeder & Water**: Ultrasonic & load cell consumption tracking.

### 4.2 Veterinary Ecosystem
Direct digital integration into veterinary practice management systems (PMLS). Guardians can generate exportable, clinical-grade PDF reports or share raw sensor timelines directly with licensed veterinarians during annual checkups.

### 4.3 Developer & OEM Ecosystem
An open **Project One SDK** permitting 3rd-party hardware companies (feeder manufacturers, door flap makers, smart collar startups) to self-describe using **PDL (Pet Description Language)** and stream events into the Project One Brain.

## 5. ENGINEERING & QUALITY CULTURE

Project One operates under the architectural rigor of aerospace and enterprise cloud infrastructure:
- **Zero Technical Debt Strategy**: System interfaces are formally specified in RFCs prior to codebase modifications.
- **2050 Longevity Constraint**: Software abstractions and protocols are version-isolated to ensure 20+ year backward compatibility.
